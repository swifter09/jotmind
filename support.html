常见问题

回顾里为什么是空的？

回顾只挑写下满一周的念头——它是用来「重新看见」的，刚写的还记得。记满一周之后，旧念头会开始以海报的样子回来。

怎么把一个念头变成「在做的」？

点念头右上角的「…」，选「孵化」；在回顾里看到时，点海报下面的「孵化」也可以。之后就能在「在做的」里加步骤、记过程。

「在做的」怎么不见了？

一件在做的事两周没有动静，会安静地退回时间线——内容一个字都没少，想做的时候再孵化就行。如果正文里写了未来的时间，比如「十月去京都」，它会一直等到那个时候，不会提前退回去。

怎么最快地记下一条？

点菜单栏里的拾念图标，或者在任何 App 里按 ⌃⌘J，都能呼出速记面板，不用切换窗口。

图片能加在哪里？

图片只能加在「在做的」的过程记录里——念头是心里的一个想法，行动才有需要留下的照片。

我的数据存在哪？怎么彻底删除？

笔记保存在你的设备上，并通过你自己的 iCloud 私有库在你的设备之间同步，开发者读不到。要彻底删除：删除 App，然后在「系统设置 → Apple 账户 → iCloud → 管理」里删除拾念的数据。详见隐私政策。

Frequently asked

Why is Review empty?

Review only picks thoughts that are at least a week old. It's there to help you see them again, and you still remember what you wrote yesterday. After a week, old thoughts start coming back as posters.

How do I turn a thought into something in progress?

Click "…" at the top right of a thought and choose "Start on it". You can also tap "Start on it" under a poster in Review. Then you can add steps and notes to it in In progress.

Where did my in-progress item go?

If nothing happens for two weeks, it quietly steps back into your timeline. Every word is still there, so you can start on it again whenever you like.

What's the fastest way to jot something down?

Click the Jotmind icon in the menu bar, or press ⌃⌘J in any app, to bring up quick capture without switching windows.

Where can I add photos?

Photos go on the notes you add to something in progress. A thought is something in your head; photos belong to what you actually do.

Where is my data, and how do I delete all of it?

Your notes are stored on your devices and synced through your own private iCloud database. The developer can't read them. To delete everything, delete the app, then remove Jotmind's data under System Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage. See the privacy policy for details.
