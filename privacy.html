拾念 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 29 日

拾念（以下简称「本应用」）是一款个人笔记应用。我们的原则很简单：你的念头是你的，我们不收集、不上传、不出售、不分析。

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。本应用没有账号系统，没有广告，没有统计分析，没有自建的崩溃收集，也没有任何第三方 SDK。开发者无法读取你记录的任何内容。

唯一的例外由 Apple 控制，而不是本应用：如果你在系统设置里选择了「与 App 开发者共享」分析数据，Apple 会向开发者提供匿名、汇总的崩溃与使用统计。这些数据不包含你的笔记内容，你可以随时在系统设置里关闭。

你的笔记存在哪里

  • 你的笔记，以及你附在笔记上的图片，保存在你自己的设备上。
  • 如果你在设备上登录了 iCloud，本应用会通过 Apple 的 CloudKit，把笔记和图片同步到你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库，以便在你的多台设备之间保持一致。这份数据存放在你的 iCloud 账户里，由 Apple 托管，开发者没有任何权限访问。你可以随时在系统设置中管理或删除它。

设备端处理

本应用的「相关的念头」「标签建议」等功能，使用 Apple 系统提供的设备端语言处理能力完成，你的念头在这个过程中不会离开你的设备。

权限

  • 本应用不请求通讯录、位置、相机、麦克风、照片图库或任何跟踪相关的权限。
  • 给笔记添加图片时，本应用使用系统自带的照片选择器或文件选择器，只会拿到你亲手选中的那几张，无法访问你的整个相册或其他文件。
  • 全局快捷键仅用于唤出速记面板，不监听你在其他应用里的输入。

儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集任何人的个人信息。

变更

如果本政策有变化，我们会在这个页面更新，并修改上方的生效日期。

Jotmind Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Jotmind is a personal notes app. Our principle is simple: your thoughts are yours. We don't collect, upload, sell, or analyze them.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting of its own, and no third-party SDKs. The developer cannot read anything you write.

The one exception is controlled by Apple, not by the app: if you choose to "Share with App Developers" in System Settings, Apple provides the developer with anonymous, aggregated crash and usage statistics. This never includes your notes, and you can turn it off at any time in System Settings.

Where your notes live

  • Your notes, and any photos you attach to them, are stored on your own devices.
  • If you're signed in to iCloud, the app uses Apple's CloudKit to sync your notes and photos to your own private iCloud database, so they stay consistent across your devices. That data lives in your iCloud account, hosted by Apple, and the developer has no access to it. You can manage or delete it at any time in System Settings.

On-device processing

Features such as "Related thoughts" and tag suggestions use Apple's on-device language processing. Your thoughts never leave your device while this happens.

Permissions

  • The app doesn't request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, your photo library, or anything used for tracking.
  • When you add a photo to a note, the app uses the system photo picker or file picker and only receives the items you choose. It can't see the rest of your library or your files.
  • The global shortcut is only used to bring up the quick-capture panel. It doesn't monitor what you type in other apps.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't collect anyone's personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
